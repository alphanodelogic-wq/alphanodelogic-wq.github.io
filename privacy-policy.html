Money Flow — Details

Money Flow is offline-first: your data lives primarily in a local database on your device, so the app works fully without internet. If you sign in with Google or your phone number, a private copy of your data is also mirrored to a cloud database (Firebase/Firestore) scoped strictly to your own account — nobody else can read it, enforced by server-side security rules keyed to your account. Using the app as a guest skips this entirely; nothing is uploaded.

Money Flow also lets you set an app-security password to protect sensitive actions (deleting data, resetting the database). This password is stored only as a salted, iterated cryptographic hash — never in plain text, and never transmitted anywhere in readable form.

Permissions Money Flow may request:

  • Biometric / device lock — optional app-lock feature; verification happens entirely on your device via the operating system, we never see or store biometric data.
  • Storage — to let you export/share a backup file or a printed receipt (PDF) that you explicitly choose to save or send.

You can edit or delete any record at any time inside the app, and you can close/delete your account from Settings, which removes your data from our cloud database.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or app data to third parties. None of our apps currently run advertising or use third-party analytics/tracking SDKs. The only outside service any of our apps currently relies on is Firebase (by Google), used by Money Flow solely for sign-in and as the private cloud backup for your own account — governed additionally by Google's Privacy Policy. We may disclose information if legally required to do so by a valid request from a public authority.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to each app — for Money Flow, this includes encrypted data in transit (TLS), server-side access rules that restrict your cloud data to your own account only, and salted/hashed storage of your security password. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we do not store more than each app genuinely needs to function.

Data Retention

Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. For Money Flow, cloud-backed data is retained for as long as your account exists and is deleted when you close/delete your account from within the app.

For Money Flow, most of this is available directly in-app (edit/delete any record, or delete your account from Settings).
